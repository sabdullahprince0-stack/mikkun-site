02 / ABOUT

WHO THE HELL
IS MIKKUN?

I’m Prince Mikael O. Sabdullah, also known as Mikkun — a multidisciplinary creative from the Philippines.

I like exploring how an idea changes when it moves between graphic design, video, branding, web, photography, and other forms of visual expression.

Most of my process begins with experimentation. I try things, rearrange them, question them, sometimes ruin them, and eventually discover what works.
